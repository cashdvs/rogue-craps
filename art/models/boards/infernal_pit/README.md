# Board A: Infernal Pit (Burn Line base board kit)

Original work by Tiny Anvil Games, generated by `src/build_board_a.py` (Blender 4.5 LTS; no third-party
meshes or textures). The preview numbers use Alfa Slab One from `fonts/` (OFL). Proprietary, same
terms as the rest of the repo (see `LICENSE`).
Spec: `art/concept/burnline_boards/board_kit.md` sections 1-4 and 7 (dimensions confirmed by Godot Dev, Oct 18, 2026).

Rebuild the meshes: `blender -b --factory-startup --python art/models/boards/infernal_pit/src/build_board_a.py`

## Space and rules
- Godot axes: metres, +Y up, -Z = far rail (target), +Z = near rail (player), scale 1, origin = felt centre, felt top y = 0.
- **Art only: no colliders, FeltDamp, scripts or animations.** The fixed physics (felt slab, rail capsules,
  backboard, containment) stays code-built; `Floor`, `Rails`, `Backboard` and `Body` only draw it.
- The rail mesh follows the collider exactly: tube radius 0.09 m, centre y = 0.02, centrelines x = +-1.11 and
  z = +-0.61, corner arcs of radius 0.09 (so the inner face meets in the same sharp corner as the two capsules).
  The tufting creases only pinch the top/outer side; the inner face keeps the 0.09 radius.
- Decoration (studs, corner caps, plates, tray, body) sits on or outside the rail centreline and under
  y = 0.25 m. Nothing sits over the felt. `tests/test_board_a_kit.gd` checks this, the anchors and the budgets.

Each glb is one mesh node at the identity, and its material slots map to `materials/<slot>.tres` through the `.import` file.

**NumberAnchor:** a `Marker3D` on the plate face. Its +Z is the face normal and +X is world right, so a
`Label3D` added as a child at the identity reads upright and faces the camera. Far plaque: face 30 deg
above horizontal. Near dial: face 60 deg above horizontal, which is the 30 deg tilt from flat.

**Anchors** (`Anchors/`, names verbatim from section 7): TargetPlate (0, 0.11, -0.61), TotalPlate (0, 0.11, 0.61),
ChipTray (1.11, 0.11, 0), BurnLine (0, 0.002, -0.40), Corner_FL/FR/NL/NR (+-1.11, 0.11, +-0.61),
RailProp_FL/FR (+-0.6, 0.11, -0.61), Zones (empty: positions come from game logic).

**Lighting:** `KeyLight` copies Lamp3D's key spot (#FFB070, 7.0, 38 deg, 1.35 m up). `RimLight` is a dim ember omni (PC only).
Remove them if the lamp stays in the table scene. No WorldEnvironment, because the table owns it.

**Not in this kit (code or loop-dependent):** the Burn Line BAND shader (Godot Dev, built), the zone decals (x2 ring,
Soul pit), ambient embers, the board/theme scripts (`board.gd`, `BoardTheme`).

**UVs:** the felt is 0-1 across the bed. Rails: U = path length / 0.5 m (a tiling leather texture), V = around the tube,
seam underneath. Props use Smart UV Project islands with 2% margin. There are no textures yet; one 1k atlas per theme can come later.
