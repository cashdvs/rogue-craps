Each `.glb.import` maps the slots to `materials/<slot>.tres` (like the Bone d6). Body slots carry the shared ink hull
(`die_ink_outline.tres`, `outline.gdshader` mode 1, 0.0042 m) as `next_pass`; the fin-read accents (spikes, beads,
fins, seals, collars) carry a thinner hull of their own (`die_ink_accent.tres`, same shader, 0.0022 m). Everything uses
`shaders/3d/toon_noir.gdshader` except the Mirror body, which uses `shaders/toon_mirror.gdshader` (below).
Rebuild: `blender -b --factory-startup --python art/models/dice/src/build_special_dice.py -- [mirror_d6 ...]`
(Blender 4.5 LTS). Batch-2 materials and import slot mapping: `python3 art/models/dice/src/write_batch2.py --imports`
(after one `godot --headless --import`, so the `.import` files exist). `tests/test_special_dice.gd` checks all of this.

## Shared conventions (same as the Bone d6 / `DieVisual`)
- Metres, +Y up, centred on the origin, one mesh node, scale 1. The 6 face planes sit at +-0.04 m (0.08 m die);
  only decoration (spikes, beads) goes past them.
- Faces: plain Bone values 1-6 on every die (`design/bonfire_build_content.md`: all special dice use plain Bone
  faces), **1 = +Y, 6 = -Y, 2 = +X, 5 = -X, 3 = +Z, 4 = -Z**, opposites sum to 7. Pips sit at
  `DieVisual.PIPS * PIP_STEP * edge` with the same u/v axes, so `value_up()` / `remap_basis()` work unchanged.
- UVs: the Bone d6's 3x2 face atlas (no textures used yet).
- No fire on any die (PM, Oct 18). Glow is material emission only (Hellfire pips/seams, bloom on emission > 1).

## Colliders (for the dev; not in the assets): box only
Godot Dev (Oct 19): every die uses the same `BoxShape3D` 0.08 m (`TuningData.die_size`), no convex hulls. The
meshes are trimmed to fit it: nothing pokes out more than ~1.8 mm past a face on any axis, so in any resting
orientation the decoration sinks at most that far into the felt. **Felt-sink ruling (Godot Dev, Oct 19):** Hellfire
and Wanderer may reach 1.08x (max 0.0432 m per axis, ~3 mm) because their spikes / fins taper to thin tips, so
what sinks is a sliver; every other die stays at 0.042 m or less. `build_special_dice.py`: `VISUAL_EXTENT_SCALE =
1.045` (0.0418 m, the default) and `EXTENT_SCALE = {"hellfire_d6": 1.08, "wanderer_d6": 1.08}` set the per-die
`BOX_HALF` that the builders and `clamp_to_box()` use. `tests/test_special_dice.gd` asserts the per-die caps (0.0432
for those two, 0.042 for the rest) and that only tapered tips pass 0.042 m (every such vertex within 5 mm of a tip).
Revisions (AD, Oct 19): Wanderer's 12 edge fins became 8 corner blades, then (v3: Hellfire owns the corners) 6 swept edge
blades; the sliver rule and cap are unchanged.

| Die | Max extent per axis (before -> after) | Box sink (before -> after) | How |
|---|---|---|---|

| Hellfire | 0.0457 -> 0.0416 -> **0.0430** | 5.7 -> 1.6 -> 3.0 mm (needle tip only) | corner spikes shortened, then (fin read) re-made as bone needles: 8.4 mm base necking to 2 mm and a point, so only the last ~1.7 mm of each needle passes 0.042 m |

| Wanderer | 0.0464 -> 0.0416 -> **0.0430** | 6.4 -> 1.6 -> 3.0 mm (blade points only) | rounder edge (0.16 x edge); (fin read v3) 6 edge blades, 46 mm along the edge and 7 mm thick at the root, raked 16 mm one way round the die and thinning to a 0.6 mm point; only the last ~3 mm (the brass tip) passes 0.042 m |
| Blood Pact | 0.0473 -> 0.0418 | 7.3 -> 1.8 mm | seals clamped to the box: the wax presses flat onto the three corner faces, bone stamp on top |

## The dice (silhouette first, then material)
**Mirror Die** (Mirror: copies the nearest die's face). Silhouette: a faceted 2-plane chamfer (bevel 0.24 x edge, flat
facets, hard-shaded), so the outline is a crisp octagon-ish block against Bone's soft round square.
Material: dark obsidian (#1A2226) with a *fake stepped reflection*: `toon_mirror.gdshader` takes the world-space
reflection vector and picks one of 4 hard bands (lamp = Bone #E8DCC0, steel #626B6D, ink horizon = Pit Black,
felt = Underfelt #123A36). On flat facets every plane flips band on its own as it tumbles: a mirror read with no
probes, SSR or gradients (Compatibility-safe). Hard lamp specular. Pips: Bone-white #E8DCC0 in 8-sided drills
(a light-on-dark die, the inverse of Bone). Params: `lamp_lo / steel_lo / horizon_lo` (band cut-offs on
reflection.y), `reflect_energy`, `lamp_energy`, band colours.

**Cracked Die** (x2 mult on its pot; may shatter). Silhouette: 4 corners broken off by two tilted planes each (a
stepped, irregular break, cut as deep as the pips allow). Material: aged bone #DCCDA6; breaks and cracks are dark
#3E3029 / Pit Black shadows (`cracked_crack`), the cracks 3 mm wide grooves that random-walk from the breaks across 5
faces, always clear of the pips (the generator routes them, seed 1666). Pips: Pit Black like Bone. The cracks
and the shatter clip (`art/vfx/bonfire` "cracked_shatter") tell the same story.

**Hellfire die**. Silhouette: a tighter-bevelled (0.10 x edge) heavy block with a 5-sided bone needle spike on all 8 corners,
the spikiest outline of the set. Material: charred charcoal #1E1B1A (Pit Black shadow, a thin ember rim), an
ember #E8622A seam glowing along the middle of all 12 edge bevels (emission 1.25), ember pips with emission 1.6
(they bloom). Spikes on all 8 corners (not horns on the top 2) so it doesn't borrow the Satanic/Mischievous
horn tier cue.

**Gilded die**. Silhouette: a brass block with a bead on every corner and every edge middle (20 studs), so the outline
is "studded" at 48 px. Material: Tarnished Brass #B08D3C body (spec #E2C06A, brass rim), each face a recessed
darker panel (#6B5A34) framed by the raised bright rim: the filigree frame. Pips: faceted ruby gems
(Hellfire red #C8281E, 6-sided cone settings, hard white glints).

## Batch 2 (silhouette first, then material)
Plain Bone faces 1-6 on all six (same pip layout / face axes as above). No fire shapes, no emission on any of them,
and no rarity cue: no brass corner caps (Devilish) and no horns (Mischievous / Satanic). Each takes a silhouette
family none of the others use: pinched waist, swept edge blades (Wanderer; Hellfire owns the corners), stepped edges, two corner seals, concave edges, collars.

**Palette** (AD, Oct 18, `/workspace/dice/art/concept/special_dice/batch2_palette.md`): spread hue AND value under the
warm lamp so no two neighbours merge at 48 px. Never Soul cyan #4FE6E0, Danger #FF2E3E, the Satanic violet glow or
felt green on a body (`tests/test_special_dice.gd` checks the body albedos and that no batch-2 slot is emissive).
The hexes below are how the die should *read*. The lamp is #FFB070 (linear blue x0.16, green x0.43), which dragged
steel blue, plum and ash all to the same beige, so `write_batch2.py`'s `lamp_comp()` pre-divides those body albedos
by part of the lamp tint and lowers `light_gain` so the lit face lands near the swatch. The `.tres` albedos are
therefore bluer than the swatches on purpose; the Blender materials keep the swatch hexes.

**Twin Flame Die** (Pair / Two Pair +4 mult). Silhouette: two dice fused at the waist. The x = 0 plane splits it,
and V-notches (Pit Black clefts) bite the 4 edges that cross the seam, so the outline pinches in the middle.
Material (the maximum value split): one half bone #EDE3CF, the other charcoal #2A2426 (`twin_flame_half`), with a
thin ember #E8622A line (`twin_flame_line`, 3.2 mm, not emissive, low light gain so it stays ember when lit) round
the seam. Pips flip colour with the half: Pit Black on bone, bone on charcoal (`twin_flame_pip_light`). The centre
pips of the 1 and 3 sit on the seam, so they are split light and dark: twins.

**Wanderer Die** (runs may skip one value). Silhouette: six fat dark-iron blades at the middles of alternating edges
(the 6 that skip the (+,+,+) / (-,-,-) corners), raked one way round the die like a compass rose / propeller, each
thinning to a small bright brass tip, 1.08x for the box collider. Hellfire owns the corners; Wanderer the edges. Material (mid value, cool): steel blue #5C7894
(desaturated, well clear of Soul cyan), dark iron #34302E blades (lamp-compensated, ~22% value as lit, inked) with bright brass #E2C06A tips, cream #F2E6C8 pips.

**Bellows Die** (Hot Hands +2 mult). Silhouette: every edge folded into a 3-step staircase (accordion pleats, steps
3 / 6 / 9.5 mm), so the outline corners are stepped, not round or chamfered. Material: tan leather #B88A5A, the
pleat risers dark brown leather #3A2418 (they draw the folds), oxblood #3B0D14 pips. Light-mid value, warm.

**Blood Pact Die** (+1 mult per 5 missing Soul). Silhouette: two black wax seals (a wide disc plus a bone stamp boss)
pressed onto opposite corners (+,+,+ / -,-,-): two lumps, asymmetric, unlike Gilded's 20 small studs.
Material (dark, warm): glossy deep crimson #7E1626 (spec + a red rim; far darker than Danger), black wax seals
#141012 glossy, each pressed flat with a wide bone #E8DCC0 stamp (`blood_pact_stamp`, the pips' bone at full value, inked) inside a black wax ring, bone pips (the signature).

**Coven Die** (Trips / Full House x2). Silhouette: all 12 edges scooped into concave coves (r 13.6 mm), leaving pointed
three-way corners. Face-on, the outline is a square with concave corners (cross-like), the inverse of Bone's
round. Material (mid-dark): matte dusty plum #6E4A6E (roughness 0.95, no spec, no glow or emission; not the
Satanic violet), matte wax-pale coves #D9CBA8 that draw a lattice around the faces, bone pips.

**Last Breath Die** (x3 when potted from a 1-2 die rethrow). Silhouette: a stone urn block with a dark iron collar
hoop (1.6 mm proud, trimmed for the box collider) round BOTH ends, outside the side pips, so it reads as a banded urn from any side and stays
centred and symmetric (see colliders). Material (light, cool): pale ash stone #9AA0A6 (lamp-compensated so it reads
grey, not Bone), dark iron #34302E collars (lamp-compensated, ~22% value as lit, with their own ink hull), charcoal #2A2426 pips.
